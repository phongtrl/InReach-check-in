    What each licence includes

    Garmin inReach Professional plans — monthly price in NOK. Every plan includes 24/7 emergency SOS and carries a one-time activation fee. A device's plan can be changed (up or down) at any check-in/out.

    Suspendkr 65/mnd
    Pauses satellite service in the off-season or when staff aren't deployed in the field, while still keeping emergency SOS available on a compatible device. Reactivate to a full plan any time — moving up or down a plan is free.
    Enabled/Basickr 170–265/mnd
    Entry-level professional service, always with 24/7 emergency SOS. Enabled (kr 170) is pay-as-you-go — check-ins, text/weather messages, photo/voice messages and live tracking are all billed per use. Basic (kr 265) adds unlimited check-ins, 50 included text/weather messages and 25 photo/voice messages; live tracking is charged per point (10-min+ intervals).
    Advancedkr 460/mnd
    Unlimited check-ins, 150 included text/weather messages and 50 photo/voice messages, plus unlimited live tracking at 10-minute (or longer) intervals. Emergency SOS included.
    Premierkr 725/mnd
    Everything unlimited — check-ins, text/weather messages, photo/voice messages and live tracking down to 2-minute intervals. Emergency SOS included. Best for continuous, heavy use.
    Deactivatedno fee
    No active subscription — the device has no satellite messaging, tracking or SOS, and no monthly cost. Reactivating a cancelled plan is subject to the one-time activation fee.
